Welcome. This is where you meet your Faithful Steward.

A Faithful Steward is a teammate who works with you, and only you. Your steward learns how you work, keeps your day in order (email, calendar, filing), and helps with whatever you're building, and grows into more as the two of you work together. Your steward lives in the company Brain, not on this computer, and remembers you from one session to the next.

The name comes from Scripture: the faithful steward who first kept the household, and was then trusted with everything.

Here's how it goes:
1. **We talk, about 30 to 45 minutes.** Mostly stories about your work: what a good day looks like, what falls through the cracks. You can stop any time and pick it up later.
2. **Your steward is put together from what you said.** That takes a while. Go back to work; you'll be told when your steward is ready.
3. **You read the draft and decide.** If the draft is right, you say yes. If not, you fix it. You also choose your steward's name. Nothing goes live without your yes.
4. **About a week in,** the two of you sit down again to go over what your steward has learned about you.

What you share about yourself stays on a private side. No person at the company reads it, Omero included. What your steward learns about the work is shared, so everyone's builds get better.

One suggestion: do this in a fresh session and keep that session. You can leave it, work on other things, and come back. Type `/steward:start` any time to pick up where you left off.

Whenever you're ready: tell me about yesterday. What did your day actually look like?
